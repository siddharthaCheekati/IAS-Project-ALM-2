Why does it work?
A = gᵃ mod p
B = gᵇ mod p

Alice: s = Bᵃ mod p
Bob:   s = Aᵇ mod p

Both:  s = gᵃᵇ mod p
These small numbers are deliberately insecure, making the mathematics easy to follow. Unauthenticated Diffie-Hellman does not prevent a man-in-the-middle attack.


HASHING & DIGITAL SIGNATURES
A fingerprint you can verify.
Hash a message with SHA-512, then create and verify a textbook ElGamal signature.
Your hash will appear here.
Inside ElGamal
p = 467, g = 2
Public key: y = gˣ mod p

r = gᵏ mod p
s = (H(m) − xr) k⁻¹ mod (p − 1)

Verify: gᴴ⁽ᵐ⁾ = yʳ rˢ mod p
The random nonce k must be coprime to p − 1. A fresh nonce is generated for every signature.
This is a small-parameter teaching implementation, not a production signature scheme. SHA-512 itself is computed with Python's hashlib.
